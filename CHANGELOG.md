## 0.1.0 - 2026-11-23
The first release.

### Added
- A popup with the apps' tray icons from the main monitor's taskbar, in "Pinned" and "Overflow", on the monitor the mouse is on. Left and right clicks on an icon reach the real one, hidden icons included.
- It opens with a hotkey (Alt+` by default; other keys can be set) or a floating button on each monitor other than the main one.
- The popup keeps up with icons that come and go while it is open.
- Drag an icon to change the popup's order within its section; Reset icon order brings back Windows' order.
- A settings window: Start with Windows, the hotkey, Reset icon order, and for each monitor its button (on or off, color, size, always on top).
- The button and the popup move together; where they were dragged is kept.
- The floating button hides while a full-screen video or game is in front on its monitor.
- Windows' notifications show on the mouse's monitor while it is on another one.
- Steam's tray menu opens at the click on monitors with other scaling.
